The right project changes everything

A good patch needs
a place to land.

A trending repository can catch your eye and still be the wrong place to contribute. Landable looks past the stars, before you spend your time.

Are new contributors welcomed?

Look at who actually gets merged and how maintainers respond. A contribution guide is a start. Recent history is evidence.

Is there a real opening for you?

Match the stack, scope, and hardware to what you can finish. Check existing claims. Follow the cause into dependencies.

Will the work belong here?

Read the project’s rules, tests, and review expectations. Propose architectural changes before building them. Respect work already claimed.
